DayTape uses the legacy online diarizer's pyannote_segmentation and wespeaker_v2 artifacts through FluidAudio. Models are downloaded when the user prepares this optional feature; no recording is sent to the model host.

pyannote_segmentation: pyannote/segmentation-3.0, upstream revision e66f3d3b.
Copyright (c) 2023 CNRS. MIT License (reproduced below).

wespeaker_v2: pyannote/wespeaker-voxceleb-resnet34-LM, upstream revision 837717dd, wrapping WeSpeaker's VoxCeleb ResNet34-LM.
Attribution: WeSpeaker, pyannote and Fluid Inference.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://huggingface.co/pyannote/wespeaker-voxceleb-resnet34-LM

Fluid Inference converted the checkpoints to Core ML, used fixed input shapes, fused the fbank frontend into the embedding model, and palettized wespeaker_v2 weights to 8 bits. DayTape uses these modified Core ML conversions and does not retrain the checkpoints. The model repository's Community-1 license scope is separate from these legacy artifacts.

WeSpeaker citation:
Wang et al. (2023), Wespeaker: A research and production oriented speaker embedding learning toolkit. ICASSP 2023.
Segmentation citation:
Plaquet and Bredin (2023), Powerset multi-class cross entropy loss for neural speaker diarization. INTERSPEECH 2023.
